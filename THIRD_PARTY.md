## Korg firmware and DataBeat OS

Korg under Korg's software licence agreement; Korg retains all rights in its
firmware. Studio ships only DataBeat OS's own changes, applies them on your
computer to your copy of Korg's updater and checks the result.

## Groove sources

The catalog contains quantized timing offsets derived from Grooves From Mars
(MPC3000), Kit Maker's J Dilla-inspired MIDI loops and the pATCHES D'Angelo
Groove Pack, plus analytical swing grids. It contains none of their audio,
MIDI or original groove files. Their names acknowledge the sources and do
not imply endorsement.
